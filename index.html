High-Yield Mnemonics & Exam Trap Radar

Instant recall cheat sheet for the most commonly failed questions on Series 17-52.

CRITICAL TRAP #1

Free Look: 10 Days vs. 30 Days

Exam Rule: Standard Individual A&H policies carry a mandatory 10-day Free Look from delivery. But Senior-targeted policies (Medicare Supplements / Medigap & Long-Term Care) mandate a 30-day Free Look!

RECALL CUE Senior Care = 30-Day Free Look | Standard = 10-Day Free Look
CRITICAL TRAP #2

Grace Period: Mode Matching

Exam Rule: The grace period depends strictly on the premium paying frequency: Weekly = 7 days, Monthly = 10 days, All other modes (Quarterly, Semi-Annual, Annual) = 31 days.

RECALL CUE GRACE 7 - 10 - 31 (Weekly 7, Monthly 10, Others 31)
CRITICAL TRAP #3

NY DBL: 26 Weeks Maximum

Exam Rule: NY Disability Benefits Law (DBL) provides cash payments for non-occupational off-the-job injuries/illness for a statutory maximum of 26 weeks in any 52-consecutive-week period (at 50% of wages up to cap).

RECALL CUE DBL = 26 WEEKS (Half a year max; zero medical benefits covered)
CRITICAL TRAP #4

Time Limit on Certain Defenses

Exam Rule: Statements made in the application become incontestable after 2 years, EXCEPT for fraudulent misstatements, which can be contested at any time.

RECALL CUE INCON-2-YEARS: 2 years for innocent errors, never expires for fraud
CRITICAL TRAP #5

Notice of Claim vs. Proof of Loss

Exam Rule: Notice of Claim must be given to insurer within 20 days. Insurer must provide claim forms within 15 days. Proof of Loss must be submitted within 90 days.

RECALL CUE NOTICE 20 -> FORMS 15 -> PROOF 90
CRITICAL TRAP #6

Legal Actions Window

Exam Rule: The insured cannot file a lawsuit against the insurer earlier than 60 days after submitting Proof of Loss, and no later than 3 years after proof was required.

RECALL CUE LEGAL ACTIONS: Wait 60 Days, Sue within 3 Years
