# GMetrix & Proficiency Curriculum: NY Series 17-52 Accident & Health Insurance
## Source ID: SRC-007 | Target: 5 Core Exam Modules

Reference URL: https://www.gmetrix.net/program/268/study-guide & Official NYDFS / PSI Candidate Information Bulletin (7228)

### Core 5 Modules:
1. FIELD UNDERWRITING PROCEDURES
   - Producer's role in field underwriting
   - Soliciting, completing, and submitting applications
   - Applicant statements, representations vs warranties (NYIL § 3105)
   - Insurable interest requirements (NYIL § 3205)
   - Underwriting information sources (Application, APS, MIB, FCRA inspection report)
   - Risk classification (Preferred, Standard, Substandard/Rated, Declined)
   - Premium receipts (Conditional receipt, Binding receipt, Approval receipt)
   - Policy delivery (Prepaid vs Non-prepaid, Statement of Good Health, Delivery date Free Look)
   - Replacement rules (NY Insurance Regulation 60, 11 NYCRR Part 51, 30-day Free Look)
   - Privacy & Fair treatment (FCRA, HIPAA, GLBA, Cybersecurity 23 NYCRR 500)

2. OTHER INSURANCE CONCEPTS
   - Risk (Pure vs Speculative), Peril vs Hazard (Physical, Moral, Morale)
   - Indemnity, Insurable Interest timing (at inception)
   - Cost sharing: Deductibles, Copayments, Coinsurance, Out-of-Pocket Maximums
   - Coordination of Benefits (COB) & Birthday Rule
   - Primary vs Secondary coverage
   - Subrogation and reimbursement rights
   - Eligibility, Dependents (Age 26 Federal vs NY Age 29 Young Adult Option), Qualifying Life Events
   - Pre-existing conditions (ACA rules: zero exclusions on essential health benefits)
   - Underwriting & Adverse Selection, Risk pooling, Reinsurance
   - Individual vs Group coverage (Master policy vs Certificate)
   - Taxation of premiums and benefits (Individual A&H, Disability Income, Group Health, BOE, Key Person)

3. POLICY PROVISIONS, CLAUSES, AND RIDERS
   - 12 Mandatory Provisions (NYIL § 3216): Entire Contract, Time Limit on Defenses (Incontestable 2 yrs), Grace Periods (7-10-31), Reinstatement (10-day sickness wait), Notice of Claim (20 days), Claim Forms (15 days), Proof of Loss (90 days), Time of Payment of Claims (immediately/30 days in NY), Payment of Claims (Facility of Payment up to $1,000), Physical Exam & Autopsy, Legal Actions (60 days to 3 years), Change of Beneficiary.
   - 11 Optional Provisions: Change of Occupation, Misstatement of Age, Other Insurance, Relation of Earnings to Insurance, Unpaid Premium, Cancellation, Conformity with State Statutes, Illegal Occupation, Intoxicants/Narcotics.
   - Renewability Spectrum: Noncancelable, Guaranteed Renewable, Conditionally Renewable, Optionally Renewable, Cancelable.
   - Riders: Waiver of Premium, Impairment Exclusion Rider, Guaranteed Insurability, COLA, SIS.

4. SOCIAL INSURANCE
   - Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI): 5-month waiting period, fully insured status (40 quarters), strict total disability definition (12 months or death).
   - Medicare Part A (Hospital), Part B (Medical/Outpatient), Part C (Medicare Advantage), Part D (Prescription).
   - Medicare Supplement (MediGap Plans A-N, NY year-round open enrollment & community rating, 30-day Free Look).
   - Medicaid (Title XIX): Means-tested, 60-month lookback for institutional care.
   - Workers' Compensation (On-the-job, medical + wage replacement) vs NY Disability Benefits Law (NY DBL off-the-job non-occupational wage replacement, 50% AWW up to $170/wk, 26 wks max, 7-day wait).
   - Long-Term Care Financing & NYS Partnership for LTC (Medicaid Total Asset Protection).
   - ACA Protections: 10 EHBs, zero annual/lifetime caps, metal tiers (BSGP 60-70-80-90%), $0 individual mandate penalty.

5. TYPES OF POLICIES
   - Individual vs Group Health Insurance
   - Fee-for-Service vs Managed Care: HMO (closed network, PCP gatekeeper, capitation), PPO (in/out network, fee-for-service, no gatekeeper), EPO (in-network only, no gatekeeper), POS (open HMO with out-of-network option).
   - Disability Income Insurance: Own Occ vs Any Occ, Elimination vs Benefit periods, Presumptive Disability.
   - Limited Policies: Accident-Only, Specified Disease / Critical Illness, Hospital Indemnity (flat daily cash benefit).
   - Dental & Vision Insurance.
   - Long-Term Care Insurance: Care levels (Skilled, Intermediate, Custodial, Respite), benefit triggers (2 of 6 ADLs BATHED or cognitive impairment).
