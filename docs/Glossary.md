## 1. Accident & Health Insurance Domain Terms

- **ADL (Activities of Daily Living)**: The 6 basic personal care activities (**BATHED**: Bathing, Ambulating, Toileting, Hygienic continence, Eating, Dressing). Inability to perform at least 2 triggers [[long-term-care-and-ny-partnership|LTC benefits]].
- **ASO (Administrative Services Only)**: Arrangement where an employer self-funds claims and hires an insurer purely for claims administration. See [[group-health-insurance-cobra-ny-continuation]].
- **BOE (Business Overhead Expense)**: Disability policy that reimburses ongoing business expenses (rent, utilities, staff salaries), but **never** the disabled owner's salary. See [[disability-income-provisions-riders-business-uses]].
- **Capitation**: Fixed monthly fee paid to an HMO provider per enrolled member regardless of whether the member receives care.
- **COB (Coordination of Benefits)**: Provision preventing double recovery when a claimant is covered under multiple group plans; uses the **Birthday Rule** for dependents. See [[group-health-insurance-cobra-ny-continuation]].
- **COLA (Cost of Living Adjustment)**: Rider that automatically increases monthly disability benefits to match inflation (CPI).
- **DBL (Disability Benefits Law)**: New York statutory program providing cash wage replacement for off-the-job injuries (50% AWW, $170/wk cap, 26 weeks, 7-day wait). Pays **$0** for medical bills. See [[new-york-disability-benefits-law-dbl]].
- **EHB (Essential Health Benefits)**: 10 mandatory categories of healthcare services that ACA individual and small-group plans must cover without annual or lifetime dollar limits. See [[affordable-care-act-aca-and-essential-health-benefits]].
- **EPO (Exclusive Provider Organization)**: Managed care plan requiring in-network care only (except emergencies), but requiring **no gatekeeper PCP referrals**. See [[medical-plans-cost-sharing-managed-care]].
- **HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)**: Closed network managed care organization utilizing capitation, a mandatory Primary Care Physician gatekeeper, and pre-authorized specialist referrals.
- **LTC (Long-Term Care)**: Insurance providing nursing, intermediate, or custodial care for chronic conditions. See [[long-term-care-and-ny-partnership]].
- **NYIL (New York Insurance Law)**: Chapter 28 of the Consolidated Laws of New York, governing all insurance activity in the state.
- **NYS DFS**: New York State Department of Financial Services, the executive agency regulating insurance, banking, and financial services in NY. See [[new-york-state-department-of-financial-services]].
- **PCP (Primary Care Physician)**: The gatekeeper doctor in an HMO or POS plan who oversees patient care and issues specialist referrals.
- **POS (Point of Service)**: Hybrid plan combining an HMO gatekeeper with the option to receive out-of-network care at higher cost-sharing.
- **PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)**: Managed care network offering discounted fee-for-service rates, out-of-network coverage, and **no gatekeeper/referral requirements**.

## 2. Knowledge Architecture & Obsidian Technical Terms

- **Backlink**: An inbound connection. A note that links *to* the currently open note.
- **CLI (Command Line Interface)**: Text-based console (PowerShell / CMD) used to run local scripts like `python scripts/wiki_lint.py`.
- **DAG (Directed Acyclic Graph)**: A mathematical network structure of nodes connected by directional edges without circular loops.
- **Frontmatter**: The YAML metadata block placed at the very top of a Markdown note between `---` delimiters.
- **Markdown**: Lightweight plaintext formatting syntax (`#`, `**`, `[[...]]`).
- **Orphan Note**: A note that has zero inbound links from any other note in the vault.
- **Wikilink**: Internal link format `[[Note Name]]` used by Obsidian to build an interconnected knowledge graph without rigid web URLs.
