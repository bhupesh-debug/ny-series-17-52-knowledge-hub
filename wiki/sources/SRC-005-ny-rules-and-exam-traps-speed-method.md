# SRC-005: New York Rules, Exam Traps & Fast Question Method (Series 17-52)

## 1. Source Metadata & Authority
- **Authority**: [Official PSI Candidate Information Bulletin](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/7228) & [NYDFS Prelicensing Topic Locator](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/11/prel_ahab_2018.pdf)
- **Topic Focus**: Wording traps, Federal vs. State distinctions, 5-Step Fast Question Method, 72-second pacing strategy, One-Minute Recall Sheet.

## 2. Core Methodologies & Rules
- **Wording Traps**:
  - *"Must," "May," "Always," "Only"*: Absolute vs. conditional statutory rules.
  - *"Best," "First," "Most Appropriate"*: Pinpointing the actual question objective.
  - *Policy Language vs. Intuition*: Contractual definitions always override personal intuition.
  - *Federal vs. NY-Specific*: ACA, HIPAA, Medicare, ERISA (Federal) vs. NY Mandates, Pure Community Rating, DBL, NY Mini-COBRA, Producer Conduct (State).
  - *Exam Series Clarification*: **17-52 is Accident & Health ONLY**; **17-55 is Combined Life and Accident & Health**.
- **Fast Question Method (72-Second Budget)**:
  1. Name the topic.
  2. Spot the decisive word.
  3. State the rule in one sentence.
  4. Cross out answers that change the rule or add an absolute.
  5. Choose the answer that fits the facts, not what sounds most generous.
