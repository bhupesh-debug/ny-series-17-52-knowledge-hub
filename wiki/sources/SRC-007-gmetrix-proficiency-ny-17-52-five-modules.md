# SRC-007: GMetrix & Proficiency Curriculum: NY Series 17-52 Accident & Health Five Modules

## 1. Source Metadata & Authority
- **Author / Institution**: GMetrix Platform (Program 268 Study Guide) & NYS Department of Financial Services (NYS DFS) / PSI Licensing Standards.
- **Publication / Verification Date**: 2026-10-28
- **Source Type**: Comprehensive Insurance Licensing Curriculum & Competency Benchmark
- **Authority Level**: Secondary Comprehensive Study Guide mapped directly to the official 100-question NY Series 17-52 Accident & Health Agent/Broker Examination.
- **Local Raw File**: `raw/documents/SRC-007-gmetrix-proficiency-ny-17-52-five-modules.md`

## 2. Executive Overview & Purpose
This source establishes the 5-module core instructional blueprint for achieving verified mastery on the NY Series 17-52 Accident & Health examination:
1. **Module 1**: Field Underwriting Procedures
2. **Module 2**: Other Insurance Concepts
3. **Module 3**: Policy Provisions, Clauses, and Riders
4. **Module 4**: Social Insurance
5. **Module 5**: Types of Policies

## 3. Module-to-Source Mapping & Statutory Authorities
- **Module 1 (Field Underwriting)**: Governed by NYIL § 2103, § 2120, § 3105 (representations), § 3205 (insurable interest), 11 NYCRR Part 51 (Reg 60 replacement), and FCRA (15 U.S.C. § 1681).
- **Module 2 (Other Insurance Concepts)**: Governed by standard contract law, indemnity doctrine, COB Model Regulation (11 NYCRR Part 52), ACA (42 U.S.C. § 18001), IRC § 104/105/106 (taxation).
- **Module 3 (Policy Provisions, Clauses & Riders)**: Governed by NYIL § 3216 (Individual A&H standard provisions), § 3221 (Group A&H provisions), 11 NYCRR Part 52.
- **Module 4 (Social Insurance)**: Governed by Social Security Act (Title II & XVIII), NY Workers' Compensation Law, NY Workers' Comp Law Article 9 (Disability Benefits Law - DBL), NYIL Article 32, and NYS Partnership for LTC (11 NYCRR Part 39).
- **Module 5 (Types of Policies)**: Governed by NYIL § 3216, § 4235 (group definition), § 4301 (non-profit health plans), 11 NYCRR Part 52.
