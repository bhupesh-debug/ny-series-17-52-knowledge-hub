## 3. Critical Up-to-Date Distinctions
- **Federal ACA Mandate**: Federal individual mandate tax penalty was reduced to **$0** beginning in 2019.
- **New York DBL**: Statutory benefit is 50% of average weekly wages, capped at **$170 per week**, for up to **26 weeks**, with a **7-day waiting period** (begins on 8th day). Applies to non-occupational (off-the-job) disability only.
- **New York Age 29 Option**: Distinct from federal ACA Age 26 rule; permits eligible young adults to continue or be added as a dependent rider up to age 29.
- **Medigap in NY**: Supplements Original Medicare (cannot be used with Medicare Advantage); subject to New York continuous open enrollment and community rating.
- **NY State Partnership for LTC**: Links qualified private LTC coverage to special Medicaid asset protection (dollar-for-dollar or total asset protection).
