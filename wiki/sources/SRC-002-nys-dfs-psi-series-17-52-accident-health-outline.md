# SRC-002: NYS DFS & PSI Series 17-52 Accident & Health Insurance Examination Content Outline

## 1. Exam Identity & Verification
- **Official Exam Title**: New York Series 17-52: Accident and Health Insurance Agent / Broker
- **Licensing Authority**: New York State Department of Financial Services (NYS DFS)
- **Exam Administrator**: PSI Services LLC
- **Scope Restriction**: **Accident & Health Insurance ONLY** (Life Insurance content is excluded from Series 17-52; Life is Series 17-51, Combined Life/Health is Series 17-55).

## 2. Exam Specifications & Structure
- **Number of Questions**: 100 Scored Multiple Choice Questions (+ 5 to 10 unscored experimental pretest questions = 105–110 total)
- **Time Allowed**: 2 Hours (120 minutes)
- **Passing Score**: **70%** (minimum 70 correct scored questions)
- **Question Format**: 4-option multiple choice (A, B, C, D), computer-administered at PSI testing centers or via remote proctor.

## 3. Official Content Outline & Topic Weights

| Section | Topic Domain | Approx. Scored Questions | Percentage Weight |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **I** | **Accident & Health Insurance Basics & Medical Plans** (Fee-for-service, HMO, PPO, EPO, POS, CDHP, HSA, FSA, HRA, Cost-sharing, Limited Policies) | ~20 questions | **20%** |
| **II** | **Individual Health Policy Provisions & Renewability** (12 Mandatory Provisions, 11 Optional Provisions, Renewability clauses) | ~25 questions | **25%** |
| **III** | **Disability Income Insurance** (Definitions of disability, elimination & benefit periods, riders, taxation, business disability) | ~15 questions | **15%** |
| **IV** | **Group & Blanket Health Insurance & ACA** (Group underwriting, COB birthday rule, COBRA, NY 36-mo continuation, ACA essential benefits) | ~15 questions | **15%** |
| **V** | **Government Programs, Senior Health & Long-Term Care** (Medicare Parts A-D, MediGap Plans A-N, Medicaid, NY LTC Partnership, Social Security Disability, Workers' Comp, NY DBL) | ~10 questions | **10%** |
| **VI** | **New York State Specific Insurance Laws & DFS Rules** (Superintendent powers, licensing § 2103/2104, 15-hr CE, rebating $25 gift rule, unfair claims, NY Life & Health Guaranty Corp) | ~15 questions | **15%** |
