# SRC-001: NYS DFS Series 17-52 Life, Accident and Health Examination Content Outline

## 1. Source Metadata & Authority
- **Institution**: New York State Department of Financial Services (NYS DFS) Licensing Bureau
- **Testing Vendor**: PSI Services LLC
- **Authority**: Highest Statutory & Administrative Authority for the New York 17-52 License Examination
- **Reference**: NY Insurance Law (NYIL) Chapter 28, Consolidated Laws of New York; Title 11 New York Codes, Rules and Operations Regulations (11 NYCRR).

## 2. Scope & Tested Domain Areas
This outline defines the legally mandated testing criteria for all candidates seeking licensure under NYIL § 2103 (Agents) and § 2104 (Brokers) for Life, Accident and Health insurance.

Key weight distribution mandated by DFS:
1. **Life Insurance Products and Underwriting**: 30%
2. **Accident and Health Insurance Products**: 28%
3. **General Insurance Principles and Contract Law**: 14%
4. **Annuities and Retirement Plans**: 8%
5. **New York Insurance Laws, DFS Rules and Ethics**: 20%

## 3. Mandatory Statutory Citations in Outline
- **NYIL § 301**: Superintendent powers and duties
- **NYIL § 309-310**: Examinations and reports on insurers
- **NYIL § 2101-2104**: Licensing definitions, exemptions, qualifications
- **NYIL § 2110**: License revocation, suspension, non-renewal, fines
- **NYIL § 2111-2117**: Acting for unauthorized insurers, temporary licenses
- **NYIL § 2120**: Fiduciary responsibilities of agents and brokers (premium accounts)
- **NYIL § 2128**: Prohibited commissions and referral fee rules
- **NYIL § 2324 & 4224**: Anti-rebating and unfair discrimination statutes
- **NYIL § 3201-3224**: Standard policy provisions (Life, Accident and Health)
- **11 NYCRR Part 51 (Insurance Regulation 60)**: Replacement of Life Insurance and Annuities
- **11 NYCRR Part 224 (Insurance Regulation 187)**: Suitability and Best Interest in Life and Annuity Transactions
- **NYIL Article 77**: Life and Health Insurance Company Guaranty Corporation of New York
