# SRC-004: NY Series 17-52: The Big Six Priority Study Framework (67% Exam Weight)

## 1. Source Metadata & Strategic Rationale
- **Authority**: [PSI Series 17-52 Outline](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/7228)
- **Core Principle**: 80/20 Rule applied to Series 17-52.
- **The "Big Six" Topics**:
  1. Medical Plans: **15%**
  2. Accident & Health Basics: **12%**
  3. Individual Health Policy Provisions: **10%**
  4. Disability Income & Related Insurance: **10%**
  5. Federal ACA: **10%**
  6. Group & Blanket Insurance: **10%**
  - **Combined Weight**: **67%** of the entire 100-question exam.
- **Remaining Topics (33%)**:
  - Government Plans: 9%
  - Insurance Regulation & Conduct: 8%
  - General Insurance: 6%
  - Long-Term Care: 5%
  - Medicare Supplements & Senior: 5%

## 2. Core Mnemonics Codified
- **D-C-C-M**: Cost Sharing (Deductible, Copay, Coinsurance, Maximum).
- **N-G-C**: Renewability (Noncancelable, Guaranteed Renewable, Cancellable).
- **E then B**: Disability (Elimination first; Benefits follow).
- **Master = Group; Certificate = Member**: Group ownership.
- **P-M-T-E**: ACA (Pre-existing conditions, Marketplace, Tax help, Essential benefits).
- **Bath, Dress, Eat, Toilet, Transfer, Control**: 6 ADLs.
- **Cause–Chance–Condition**: Peril causes; Risk is chance; Hazard is condition.
