# Medicare Supplements (MediGap) & Senior Coverage (Official 5% Weight)

## 1. Plain-English Concept (For a 10-Year-Old)
Original Medicare (Part A & Part B) pays a lot of hospital and doctor bills for seniors, but it leaves "potholes" called gaps—like deductibles and 20% coinsurance.
- **MediGap (Medicare Supplement)** is a private insurance patch that covers those potholes.
- **The Core Rule**: MediGap **ONLY works with Original Medicare (Parts A & B)**.
- If a senior decides to enroll in **Medicare Advantage (Part C)** instead, they **CANNOT use MediGap** to pay Medicare Advantage copays! In fact, it is illegal to sell a MediGap policy to someone who has Medicare Advantage unless they are switching back to Original Medicare!

---

## 2. Core Rules & Distinctions
- **Standardized Plans (Plans A through N)**:
  - Lettered plans are identical across all insurance companies (e.g. Plan G from Company X has the exact same benefits as Plan G from Company Y; only prices differ).
  - **Plan A is the Core Basic Benefit Plan**: Every insurer selling MediGap must offer Plan A.
- **New York State Specific MediGap Rules (CRITICAL NY DISTINCTION)**:
  - *Federal Rule*: 6-month open enrollment period upon turning 65 and enrolling in Part B.
  - *New York State Rule*: **Continuous, year-round open enrollment and pure community rating**! Insurers selling MediGap in New York cannot deny coverage or charge higher premiums based on age, gender, or health status at ANY time of the year!
- **Free-Look Period**: **30 Days** mandatory right of examination in New York with a 100% full refund.
- **Pre-existing Condition Waiting Period in NY**: Maximum of **6 months**, which must be credited or waived if the individual had continuous prior creditable coverage.

---

## 3. Recall Cue
- **Medigap fills gaps in Original Medicare; Advantage replaces that route.**
- **Plan A = Core Basic Benefits (Mandatory offer).**
- **New York = Year-round continuous open enrollment & pure community rating.**

## 4. Tricky Exam Scenario & Trap Analysis
> **Question**: A client enrolls in a private Medicare Advantage plan (Part C) and asks an insurance agent to sell them a MediGap policy to pay the Medicare Advantage plan's specialist copayments. What is the most accurate response?
> - A) MediGap is designed to supplement Original Medicare, not Medicare Advantage.
> - B) MediGap automatically pays all Medicare Advantage copayments.
> - C) MediGap replaces Part D prescription drug coverage.
> - D) Medicare Advantage and MediGap are interchangeable names for the same program.
>
> **Correct Answer**: **A) MediGap is designed to supplement Original Medicare, not Medicare Advantage.**
> - **Explanation**: MediGap policies work exclusively with Original Medicare (Parts A & B) to fill cost-sharing gaps. It cannot be used to pay copayments under Medicare Advantage.
> - **Tempting Wrong Answer**: **B** is tempting if candidates believe MediGap pays copayments for any health plan.
