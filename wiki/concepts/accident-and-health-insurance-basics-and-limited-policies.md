# Accident & Health Insurance Basics & Limited Policies (Official 12% Weight)

## 1. Plain-English Concept (For a 10-Year-Old)
Accident and health insurance protects you when your body breaks down or gets hurt:
- **Comprehensive Coverage (The Giant Umbrella)**: Major medical plans that pay for almost all hospital, doctor, surgery, and medicine costs.
- **Limited Coverage (The Tiny Cup)**: Policies that pay cash **only if one specific named event happens**.
  - *Hospital Indemnity*: Pays a flat cash amount (e.g., $300 for every day you are in a hospital bed), no matter how big or small the actual hospital bill is.
  - *Specified / Dread Disease (e.g. Cancer Policy)*: Pays only if you get the exact disease named in the title. If you get a stroke instead of cancer, it pays **$0**.
  - *Critical Rule*: Limited policies are **never** a substitute for comprehensive major medical insurance!

---

## 2. Core Rules & Distinctions
- **Peril vs. Loss**:
  - *Peril*: Cause of loss (injury or sickness).
  - *Loss*: The measurable financial consequence (medical bill or lost paycheck).
- **Accidental Bodily Injury vs. Accidental Means**:
  - *Accidental Bodily Injury (Result)*: Unforeseen and unintended injury (most liberal standard).
  - *Accidental Means (Cause)*: Both the cause and the result must be accidental. (Rare in modern policies; prohibited or restricted in NY).
- **Medical Necessity**:
  - Care must meet clinical standards defined in the contract. A physician's recommendation alone **does not guarantee insurance payment** unless it meets plan criteria.
- **Common Statutory & Contractual Exclusions**:
  - Injuries covered by Workers' Compensation.
  - Elective cosmetic surgery (unless reconstructive following accident/mastectomy).
  - Experimental or investigational treatments.
  - Self-inflicted injuries or injuries sustained in the commission of a felony.

---

## 3. Producer Sales, Delivery & Replacement Rules (NY Tested)
- **Outline of Coverage**: Must be delivered to the applicant at the time of application or policy delivery to summarize benefits, exclusions, and renewability.
- **Errors & Omissions (E&O)**: Professional liability insurance protecting producers against claims of negligence, mistake, or failing to procure coverage.
- **Replacement Standards**: Producer must compare coverage, exclusions, waiting periods, and pre-existing condition rights, ensuring the client is not misled.

---

## 4. Recall Cue
- **"Named event, named benefit" for limited policies.**
- **Hospital indemnity pays stated cash, not the hospital's bill.**

---

## 5. Tricky Exam Scenario & Trap Analysis
> **Question**: Lee has a hospital-indemnity policy paying $300 per covered hospital day. The hospital charges $2,400 for a day of inpatient care. What does the policy generally pay?
> - A) The full $2,400
> - B) $300, if the policy's trigger and conditions are met
> - C) The bill minus the health plan deductible
> - D) Nothing unless Lee has no other insurance
>
> **Correct Answer**: **B) $300, if the policy's trigger and conditions are met.**
> - **Explanation**: A stated indemnity benefit is paid according to the fixed policy schedule ($300/day), completely independent of the actual provider charges or other insurance coverage.
> - **Tempting Wrong Answer**: **A** or **C** are tempting if candidates mistake hospital indemnity for major medical expense reimbursement.
