# 12 Mandatory & 11 Optional Health Policy Provisions

## 1. Executive Summary
Under the **NAIC Uniform Individual Accident and Sickness Policy Provisions Law** (codified in New York under **NYIL § 3216**), every individual health and accident policy issued in New York must include **12 Mandatory Provisions** to protect the insured. Insurers may also elect to include any of the **11 Optional Provisions**, but cannot alter wording to be less favorable to the insured.

---

## 2. The 12 Mandatory Health Policy Provisions (HIGH YIELD TIME LIMITS)

| # | Provision | NY Statutory Rule / Specific Time Limit | Core Mechanism & Exam Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Entire Contract; Changes** | Policy + Application + Riders = Entire Contract | Agent has NO authority to change policy or waive provisions. Only an executive officer can approve amendments. |
| **2** | **Time Limit on Certain Defenses** | **2 Years** (analogous to Incontestable Clause in life) | After 2 years, no misstatements (except fraudulent misstatements) on application can void policy or deny claim. Pre-existing condition limits also capped. |
| **3** | **Grace Period** | **7 days** (weekly premium)<br>**10 days** (monthly premium)<br>**31 days** (all other modes: quarterly, semi, annual) | *Crucial numbers*: Remember **7 - 10 - 31**. Coverage continues during grace period; overdue premium deducted from claim. |
| **4** | **Reinstatement** | **Automatic if not rejected within 45 days** | Application submitted with premium; if insurer takes no action, reinstated on 45th day. **Accidents covered immediately; Sickness covered after 10-day probationary period** (prevents adverse selection). |
| **5** | **Notice of Claim** | Within **20 days** of loss | Insured must notify insurer of loss within 20 days or as soon as reasonably possible. |
| **6** | **Claim Forms** | Within **15 days** of notice | Insurer must furnish claim forms to insured within 15 days of receiving notice. If insurer fails, insured can submit proof of loss in any written format. |
| **7** | **Proof of Loss** | Within **90 days** of loss | Written proof of loss must be filed within 90 days (not to exceed 1 year unless legally incapacitated). |
| **8** | **Time of Payment of Claims** | **Immediately** (or periodic for disability, min monthly) | Claims for losses must be paid immediately upon receipt of due written proof. Disability income benefits must be paid at least monthly. |
| **9** | **Payment of Claims** | To Insured or Beneficiary | Death benefits paid to named beneficiary or estate. Facility of payment clause allows insurer to pay up to $1,000 to a relative who incurred expenses. |
| **10** | **Physical Exam and Autopsy** | Insurer’s expense | Insurer has right to examine insured as often as reasonably necessary during pending claim, and conduct autopsy unless forbidden by law/religion. |
| **11** | **Legal Actions** | **Earliest: 60 days** after proof of loss<br>**Latest: 3 years** (or in NY, up to 2-3 years depending on form) | Insured cannot sue insurer before 60 days after submitting proof of loss; cannot sue after 3 years. |
| **12** | **Change of Beneficiary** | Revocable (default) vs. Irrevocable | Policyowner may change revocable beneficiary at any time without consent. Irrevocable requires beneficiary's written consent. |

## 3. The 11 Optional Health Policy Provisions

1. **Change of Occupation**: If insured changes to a *more hazardous* job, benefits are reduced to what premium would have bought. If changed to *less hazardous*, premiums are reduced and excess unearned premium is refunded.
2. **Misstatement of Age**: Benefits adjusted to what premium paid would have purchased at the true age.
3. **Other Insurance in This Insurer**: Prevents overinsurance; excess coverage void and premiums refunded.
4. **Insurance with Other Insurers (Expense Incurred)**: Benefits prorated with other policies; prevents profiting from loss.
5. **Insurance with Other Insurers (Other Benefits)**: Same pro-rata reduction for non-expense policies.
6. **Relation of Earnings to Insurance (Average Earnings Clause)**: In disability policies, limits disability benefit to insured's average monthly earnings over previous 2 years.
7. **Unpaid Premium**: Deducted from claim payment.
8. **Cancellation**: In New York, insurer cannot arbitrarily cancel except on renewal or nonpayment, subject to state notice requirements (generally 30 days).
9. **Conformity with State Statutes**: Any provision in conflict with New York law is automatically amended to conform to the minimum statutory requirement.
10. **Illegal Occupation**: Insurer not liable for loss resulting from insured's commission of a felony or illegal occupation.
11. **Intoxicants and Narcotics**: Insurer not liable for loss sustained while insured is intoxicated or under narcotics unless administered on advice of a physician.

## 4. Exam Traps & Multiple Choice Distractors

> [!WARNING]
> **Trap 1: Reinstatement Sickness Waiting Period**
> When a lapsed health policy is reinstated:
> - Accident coverage is effective: **IMMEDIATELY**.
> - Sickness coverage is effective: **AFTER 10 DAYS**.
> *(Examiners frequently ask this to test anti-selection knowledge!)*
>
> **Trap 2: Grace Period Matrix**
> - Weekly premium mode = **7 days**
> - Monthly premium mode = **10 days**
> - All other modes (Quarterly, Annual) = **31 days**
>
> **Trap 3: Claims Timeline Sequence (Mnemonic: "20 - 15 - 90 - 60 - 3")**
> - Notice of Claim = **20 days**
> - Company sends claim forms = **15 days**
> - Insured submits Proof of Loss = **90 days**
> - Waiting time before filing Legal Action = **60 days**
> - Maximum time limit to file Legal Action = **3 years**
