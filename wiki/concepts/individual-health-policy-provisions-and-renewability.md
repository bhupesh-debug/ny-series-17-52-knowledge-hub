# Individual Health Policy Provisions & Renewability (Official 10% Weight)

## 1. Plain-English Concept (For a 10-Year-Old)
Policy provisions are the contract rules that explain who has rights and who has duties.
- **Renewability Clauses (The Job Security of Your Policy)**:
  - *Noncancelable (Ironclad)*: The company cannot cancel your policy, and they **cannot raise your price ever**!
  - *Guaranteed Renewable (Very Good)*: The company cannot cancel your policy as long as you pay premiums, but **they CAN raise your price if they raise it for everyone in your rating class** statewide.
  - *Conditionally Renewable (Conditional)*: The company can stop renewing only if a specific event mentioned in the policy happens (e.g. you reach age 65 or lose your job).
  - *Optionally Renewable (Weak)*: The company can decide every year whether they feel like keeping you as a customer.
  - *Cancelable (Weakest)*: The company can drop you anytime with written notice.

---

## 2. Core Mandatory Provisions (Uniform NAIC & NYIL § 3216)
- **Entire Contract**: Policy + attached application + endorsements. Only an executive officer of the insurer can change terms; the agent has **zero authority** to waive or alter provisions.
- **Time Limit on Certain Defenses**: **2 Years** (analogous to life incontestability). After 2 years, no misstatements (except fraud) can void coverage or deny claims.
- **Grace Periods**: **7 days** (weekly premium), **10 days** (monthly premium), **31 days** (all other premium modes).
- **Reinstatement**: Reinstatement is automatic on the **45th day** if insurer takes no action. Accidents covered immediately; **sickness covered after a 10-day probationary period**.
- **Claims Countdown (20-15-90-60-3)**:
  - *Notice of Claim*: 20 days.
  - *Claim Forms*: 15 days.
  - *Proof of Loss*: 90 days.
  - *Time of Payment*: Immediately upon proof.
  - *Legal Actions*: Minimum wait of 60 days after proof of loss before filing suit; maximum 3 years to initiate legal action.

---

## 3. Coordination of Benefits (COB)
- Coordinates payments when an individual is covered under more than one group plan.
- Designed to establish primary vs. secondary payer order and **prevent double recovery (profiting from a loss)**.

---

## 4. Recall Cue
- **"Noncancelable locks cancellation and rate; guaranteed renewable locks renewal."**
- **Grace periods = 7 - 10 - 31.**
- **Reinstatement = 10-day sickness wait; accidents immediate.**

---

## 5. Tricky Exam Scenario & Trap Analysis
> **Question**: An insurer may raise a guaranteed-renewable policy's premium for all policyholders in a permitted class, but cannot refuse to renew an individual insured just because that person became ill. Which provision is this?
> - A) Noncancelable
> - B) Guaranteed renewable
> - C) Optionally renewable
> - D) Nonrenewable
>
> **Correct Answer**: **B) Guaranteed renewable**
> - **Explanation**: Guaranteed renewable contracts guarantee the right of the insured to renew the policy up to a specified age (often 65), but reserve the insurer's right to adjust premium rates on a class-wide basis.
> - **Tempting Wrong Answer**: **A (Noncancelable)** is tempting, but noncancelable policies forbid ANY premium increases.
