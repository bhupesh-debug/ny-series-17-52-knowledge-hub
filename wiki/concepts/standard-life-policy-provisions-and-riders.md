# Standard Life Policy Provisions, Options & Riders

## 1. Executive Summary
Under **New York Insurance Law (NYIL § 3203)**, every life insurance policy delivered or issued for delivery in New York must contain specific mandatory standard provisions designed to protect consumers. Furthermore, policies can be customized using Nonforfeiture Options, Dividend Options, Settlement Options, and Policy Riders.

---

## 2. Mandatory Life Policy Provisions (NYIL § 3203)

| Provision | Timeframe / Rule in New York | Core Mechanism | Exam Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Entire Contract** | Policy + attached application + endorsements | Prevents insurer from referencing outside documents, bylaws, or unattached rules. | Insurer cannot alter terms after issue without policyowner consent. |
| **Incontestability Clause** | **2 Years** from policy date of issue | After 2 years, insurer cannot contest or void policy for misstatements (even fraudulent ones, with rare criminal impersonation exceptions). | During first 2 years, contestable for material misrepresentations. |
| **Grace Period** | **31 days** (or 1 month) | Protects against unintentional lapse. Coverage remains in force. If insured dies during grace period, death benefit is paid **minus past due premium**. | Grace period does NOT give free insurance forever; dies in grace = pays death benefit minus owed premium. |
| **Reinstatement** | Up to **3 years** (in some contracts 5 years) | Allows restoring a lapsed policy. Requires: (1) Evidence of insurability, (2) Payment of all back premiums + interest, (3) Repayment of policy loans. A surrendered policy **cannot** be reinstated. | A policy surrendered for cash value is permanently terminated; CANNOT reinstate! |
| **Misstatement of Age and Sex** | Incontestable clause does NOT apply | If age/sex is misstated, insurer adjusts death benefit to what premiums paid would have purchased at the correct age/sex. | Insurer NEVER voids the policy for misstated age; they adjust the benefit payout. |
| **Suicide Clause** | **2 Years** in New York | If insured commits suicide within first 2 years: insurer refunds **premiums paid without interest** (no death benefit). After 2 years: pays full death benefit. | Many think it voids coverage forever; after 2 years, suicide is fully covered! |
| **Free-Look (Right to Examine)** | **10 to 30 days** in NY (10 days standard; **30 days for replacement / mail order / senior**) | Policyowner may return policy for a 100% full refund of all premiums paid without reason. | Under NY [[new-york-insurance-regulation-60-replacement|Regulation 60]], replacement free look is **30 days**! |
| **Policy Loans** | Available after **3 years** of cash value | Policyowner can borrow against cash value. Maximum fixed loan interest rate in NY is **7.4%** or an approved variable index. Loan reduces death benefit if unpaid. | Loans are not taxable up to basis unless policy is a Modified Endowment Contract (MEC). |

## 3. Nonforfeiture Options (Mnemonic: "CRE")
Required in cash value policies after 3 years. Protects equity if policyowner stops paying premiums:

1. **Cash Surrender Value**:
   - Policy is surrendered; insurer sends check for net cash value minus surrender charges/loans.
   - Policy is canceled permanently; cannot be reinstated.
2. **Reduced Paid-Up Insurance**:
   - Cash value is used as a single net premium to purchase a completely paid-up whole life policy of a **lesser face amount**.
   - No more premiums due ever. Accumulates cash value until age 100 or death.
3. **Extended Term Insurance (AUTOMATIC / DEFAULT OPTION)**:
   - Cash value is used as a single net premium to buy a term policy with the **same face amount** as the original policy for as long a duration as the cash value will buy.
   - Provides highest death benefit protection.
   - *Trap*: If the policyowner fails to choose an option, New York law makes **Extended Term** the automatic default!

---

## 4. Dividend Options (Participating Policies - Mnemonic: "CRAP-O")
Dividends are a return of unearned premium; **not taxable as income**.
- **C**ash: Insurer sends check.
- **R**eduction of Premium: Applied toward next year's premium.
- **A**ccumulate at Interest: Insurer holds cash; interest earned IS taxable.
- **P**aid-Up Additions: Single premium buys additional small paid-up whole life chunks. Increases total death benefit and cash value. (Default if not chosen).
- **O**ne-Year Term: Single premium buys 1-year term insurance up to cash value or maximum allowed.

---

## 5. Settlement Options (Payment of Death Proceeds)
- **Lump Sum**: Default option; received income tax-free.
- **Interest Only**: Insurer holds death benefit; pays guaranteed interest to beneficiary periodically. Beneficiary can withdraw principal later.
- **Fixed Period**: Proceeds paid in installments over a specified number of years.
- **Fixed Amount**: Specified dollar amount paid monthly until principal and interest are exhausted.
- **Life Income (Annuity options)**: Guaranteed income for life (Straight Life, Life with Period Certain, Joint & Survivor).

---

## 6. Popular Life Insurance Riders
- **Waiver of Premium**: Waives future premiums if insured becomes totally and permanently disabled. Has a **6-month waiting period**; if still disabled after 6 months, premiums paid during waiting period are refunded. Rider expires at age 60 or 65.
- **Guaranteed Insurability Rider (GIR)**: Allows insured to purchase specified amounts of additional life insurance at specified ages (typically ages 25, 28, 31, 34, 37, 40) or marriage/childbirth **WITHOUT evidence of insurability** (no medical exam).
- **Accidental Death Benefit (Double Indemnity)**: Pays double (or triple) face amount if death occurs as a direct result of an accident within **90 days** of the accident. Does not apply to suicide, war, or illness.
- **Accelerated Death Benefit (Living Needs Rider)**: Advances up to 50% to 100% of death benefit if insured is diagnosed with a terminal illness with life expectancy of 24 months or less. Benefit paid tax-free; remaining death benefit is reduced by amount paid.
