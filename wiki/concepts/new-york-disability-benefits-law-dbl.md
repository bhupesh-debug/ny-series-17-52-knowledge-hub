# New York Disability Benefits Law (NY DBL) vs. Workers' Compensation

## 1. Plain-English Concept (For a 10-Year-Old)
- **Workers' Compensation (ON the Job)**: You slip and fall while mopping the floor *at work*. Workers' Comp pays 100% of your hospital bills PLUS replaces part of your lost wages, because the injury happened on the job!
- **New York DBL (OFF the Job)**: You go skiing on Sunday with your friends and break your leg. You can't go to work on Monday. Workers' Comp pays $0 because it didn't happen at work. But in New York, we have a special law called **NY DBL**! It pays you a temporary cash check to help you survive while you recover at home.
- *Notice*: DBL is for **OFF-THE-JOB (non-occupational)** injury or illness only!

---

## 2. Core Differences: NY DBL vs. Workers' Compensation

| Feature | Workers' Compensation | New York Disability Benefits Law (NY DBL) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Type of Injury / Sickness** | **Occupational** (Occurs ON the job / arises out of employment) | **Non-Occupational** (Occurs OFF the job, e.g. weekend accident, illness) |
| **Statutory Mandate in NY** | Mandatory for virtually all employers | Mandatory for all "Covered Employers" (1+ employees for at least 30 days in a calendar year) |
| **Medical Expenses Covered?** | **YES** (Unlimited 100% medical, hospital, rehab expenses) | **NO!** DBL provides **CASH WAGE REPLACEMENT ONLY**. It does NOT pay hospital or doctor bills! |
| **Waiting Period** | 7 calendar days (if disabled > 14 days, first 7 days are retroactively paid) | **7 consecutive days** of disability (Benefits begin on the 8th day) |
| **Maximum Benefit Duration** | As long as temporary total disability lasts, or permanent impairment schedule | Up to **26 weeks** in any consecutive 52-week period |
| **Benefit Amount** | Typically 2/3 (66 2/3%) of average weekly wage | **50% of average weekly wage**, up to a statutory state cap (currently $170/week minimum statutory base, though many employers buy enriched DBL plans) |
| **Employee Contribution** | Employer pays 100% of Workers' Comp premium; employee pays $0 | Employer may deduct up to **0.5% of wages** (maximum **$0.60 per week**) from employee paycheck |

## 3. NY Paid Family Leave (PFL) Integration
- New York integrates Paid Family Leave into the statutory DBL framework.
- Covers bonding with a new child, caring for a family member with a serious health condition, or exigency related to military deployment.
- Provides up to **12 weeks** of job-protected paid leave at **67% of average weekly wage** (subject to statewide average wage cap).
- Employee funded through payroll deduction.

## 4. Exam Traps & Multiple Choice Distractors

> [!WARNING]
> **Trap 1: Medical Bills under NY DBL**
> Does NY DBL pay doctor bills or hospital stays?
> - **ABSOLUTELY NOT!** NY DBL is **strictly cash wage replacement**. Major medical insurance pays hospital bills; DBL pays a partial weekly paycheck.
>
> **Trap 2: Waiting Period & Maximum Duration in NY DBL**
> - Waiting period before DBL cash benefits start: **7 days** (benefits start on day 8).
> - Maximum duration of DBL benefits: **26 weeks** in any 52-week period.

## 5. Exam Scenario Question & Trap Breakdown

> **Scenario**: An employee working in Syracuse, NY falls off a ladder while painting their own personal home on a Saturday afternoon and breaks their arm, missing 10 weeks of work. Which program provides statutory disability income benefits?
> - A) Workers' Compensation.
> - B) New York Disability Benefits Law (DBL).
> - C) Social Security Disability Income (SSDI).
> - D) Medicaid.
>
> **Correct Answer**: **B) New York Disability Benefits Law (DBL).**
> - **Rationale**: The injury occurred off-duty (non-occupational), which is the exact scope of NY DBL. Workers' Comp covers occupational injuries only. Social Security requires a disability expected to last at least 12 months or result in death with a 5-month waiting period.
> - **Tempting Wrong Answer**: **A** is tempting if the candidate forgets that Workers' Comp is strictly for on-the-job injuries.
