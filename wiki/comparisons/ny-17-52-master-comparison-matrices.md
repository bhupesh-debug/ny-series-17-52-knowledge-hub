# NY Series 17-52 Accident & Health Master Comparison Matrices

## 1. Terminology Comparisons (Commonly Confused Concepts)

| Concept Pair | Distinguishing Element | Legal / Financial Impact | Common Exam Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Peril vs. Hazard** | **Peril** is the *cause* of loss (sickness, fire). **Hazard** is a condition that *increases the likelihood* of peril (slippery ice, smoking). | Perils trigger policy benefits; hazards affect underwriting and rating. | Confusing moral hazard (dishonesty) with morale hazard (carelessness). |
| **Warranty vs. Representation** | **Warranty** is guaranteed literally true; breach voids contract automatically. **Representation** is believed true to best of knowledge; voids contract only if *material*. | All statements on NY insurance applications are representations (NYIL § 3105). | Believing that applicant statements in NY are warranties. They are NOT. |
| **Deductible vs. Coinsurance** | **Deductible** is a fixed dollar threshold paid *first*. **Coinsurance** is a percentage sharing of remaining covered costs (e.g. 80/20). | Deductible must be satisfied before coinsurance applies. | Calculating coinsurance before subtracting the deductible. Deductible ALWAYS comes first! |
| **Own Occ vs. Any Occ** | **Own Occ**: Unable to perform duties of own specific job. **Any Occ**: Unable to perform any job suited by training/education. | Own Occ is easier to claim and costs more; Any Occ is stricter and costs less. | Thinking Own Occ requires total bed confinement; it only requires inability to do one's own career. |

## 2. Managed Care Comparison Matrix (HMO vs. PPO vs. EPO vs. POS)

| Feature | Health Maintenance Org (HMO) | Preferred Provider Org (PPO) | Exclusive Provider Org (EPO) | Point-of-Service (POS) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Network Structure** | Closed network | In-network & Out-of-network | In-network only | Tiered (In & Out of network) |
| **PCP Gatekeeper** | **Mandatory** | **None** (direct access) | **None** (direct access) | **Mandatory** for in-network |
| **Specialist Referral** | Required from PCP | No referral needed | No referral needed | Required for in-network care |
| **Out-of-Network Coverage** | **Zero** (Emergency only) | **Yes** (at higher coinsurance/deductible) | **Zero** (Emergency only) | **Yes** (higher out-of-pocket costs) |
| **Provider Payment** | **Capitation** (flat monthly per member) | **Discounted Fee-for-Service** | Discounted Fee-for-Service | Capitation in-network; Fee-for-service out |
| **Core Philosophy** | Preventive care & cost containment | Freedom of provider choice | Lower cost with direct specialist access | Hybrid flexibility |

## 3. Social Insurance Comparison: Workers' Comp vs. NY DBL vs. SSDI

| Dimension | Workers' Compensation | New York Disability Benefits Law (NY DBL) | Social Security Disability (SSDI) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Type of Disability** | **Occupational (On-the-job)** accident or illness | **Non-Occupational (Off-the-job)** sickness or off-duty injury | Total & Permanent Disability (any cause) |
| **Medical Treatment** | **Unlimited 100% medical coverage** | **NO medical benefits** (cash replacement only) | Medicare Part A & B after **24 months** of SSDI |
| **Cash Wage Benefit** | 66 2/3% of Average Weekly Wage (AWW) | **50% of AWW up to $170/week maximum** | Primary Insurance Amount (PIA) based on earnings |
| **Duration Limit** | Permanent or temporary as determined by board | **26 weeks maximum** in any 52-week period | Until death, retirement age, or recovery |
| **Waiting Period** | 7 days (retroactive if disabled > 14 days) | **7 days** (benefits start on day 8) | **5 full calendar months** (benefits start month 6) |
| **Funding** | 100% employer-paid (or state insurance fund) | Joint: Employer + Employee (max employee 60¢/wk) | FICA payroll tax (equal employee & employer split) |

## 4. Senior Products: Medicare Supplement (MediGap) vs. Medicare Advantage (Part C)

| Feature | Medicare Supplement (MediGap) | Medicare Advantage (Part C) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Underlying System** | Supplements **Original Medicare (Parts A & B)** | **Replaces** Original Medicare |
| **Primary Payor** | Original Medicare pays primary; MediGap pays secondary | Private Medicare Advantage plan pays directly |
| **Standardized Plans** | Standardized **Plans A through N** | Plan design varies by private HMO/PPO carrier |
| **Doctor / Hospital Choice** | **Any provider nationwide** accepting Medicare | **Restricted network** (HMO/PPO service area) |
| **Prescription Drug (Rx)** | Cannot include Rx (must buy standalone Part D) | Usually bundled into plan (MA-PD) |
| **New York State Specifics** | **Continuous year-round open enrollment** and **pure community rating** | Annual Election Period (Oct 15 - Dec 7) |
| **Co-Existence Rule** | **ILLEGAL** to sell MediGap to someone enrolled in Part C | Cannot be enrolled in both simultaneously |
