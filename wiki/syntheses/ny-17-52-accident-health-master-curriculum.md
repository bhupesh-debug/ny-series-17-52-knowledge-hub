## 2. Topic-by-Topic Plain English Guide & Exam Traps

### A. Medical Plans & Cost Sharing (~20% Weight)
- **Concept**: HMO (gatekeeper/capitation/in-network only); PPO (no gatekeeper/in & out network); EPO (no gatekeeper/in-network only); POS (hybrid); Indemnity (fee-for-service reimbursement).
- **Cost Sharing**: Deductible (paid first) $\rightarrow$ Coinsurance (split e.g. 80/20) $\rightarrow$ Stop-Loss Out-of-Pocket Cap (plan pays 100% afterwards).
- **Limited Policies**: Dread disease covers named illness; Hospital indemnity pays flat daily cash directly to patient; AD&D (Principal sum for death, Capital sum 50% for 1 limb).
- **NY Specific**: Pure Community Rating for individual and small groups; pre-existing condition exclusions prohibited.
- **Detailed Note**: [[medical-plans-cost-sharing-managed-care]]

### B. Individual Health Provisions & Renewability (~25% Weight - HIGHEST)
- **12 Mandatory Provisions**: Entire contract (agent cannot change); Time limit on defenses (2 years); Grace periods (**7-10-31 days**); Reinstatement (**10-day sickness probationary period**); Claims sequence (**20d notice $\rightarrow$ 15d forms $\rightarrow$ 90d proof $\rightarrow$ 60d wait to sue $\rightarrow$ 3yr max to sue**).
- **Renewability Spectrum (From Best to Worst)**:
  1. **Noncancelable (Noncan)**: Insurer CANNOT cancel AND CANNOT raise premium rates ever! (Best & most expensive).
  2. **Guaranteed Renewable**: Insurer CANNOT cancel, BUT CAN raise premiums on a **class-wide basis** (statewide). All LTC and ACA plans must be at least guaranteed renewable.
  3. **Conditionally Renewable**: Insurer can terminate ONLY if specific contract conditions occur (e.g., reaching age 65 or leaving employment).
  4. **Optionally Renewable**: Insurer can choose not to renew on any policy anniversary date for any class.
  5. **Cancelable**: Insurer can cancel at any time with statutory written notice.
- **Detailed Note**: [[mandatory-and-optional-health-provisions]]

### C. Disability Income Insurance (~15% Weight)
- **Own Occ vs. Any Occ**: Own Occ pays if you can't do your specific regular job (liberal); Any Occ pays only if you can't do any job suited to education/training (strict).
- **Elimination vs. Benefit Period**: Elimination period = time deductible (longer = lower premium); Benefit period = duration of payout (longer = higher premium).
- **Business Disability**: BOE pays rent and employee salaries (not owner salary, benefit taxable, expenses deductible); Key Person & Buy-Out (premiums not deductible, benefits 100% tax-free).
- **Detailed Note**: [[disability-income-provisions-riders-business-uses]]

### D. Group Health Insurance, ACA & COBRA (~15% Weight)
- **Contributory vs. Noncontributory**: Contributory requires 75% participation; Noncontributory requires 100% participation (employer pays 100%).
- **Coordination of Benefits (COB)**: Birthday Rule determines primary plan for dependents (earliest birthday in calendar year, NOT older age!).
- **COBRA vs. NY Mini-COBRA**: Federal applies to 20+ workers (18 mos termination, 36 mos divorce/death); **New York Mini-COBRA applies to 1–19 workers and extends coverage up to 36 months**!
- **ACA Metal Tiers**: Bronze 60%, Silver 70%, Gold 80%, Platinum 90% actuarial value. Dependent coverage to age 26 (NY Age 29 law).
- **Detailed Notes**: [[group-health-insurance-cobra-ny-continuation]], [[affordable-care-act-aca-and-essential-health-benefits]]

### E. Government Programs, Senior Health & Long-Term Care (~10% Weight)
- **Medicare**: Part A (Inpatient hospital, 60/90/150 days); Part B (Outpatient, 80/20 coinsurance); Part C (Medicare Advantage); Part D (Prescription drugs).
- **MediGap**: Standardized Plans A–N. **New York requires year-round continuous open enrollment and community rating** (no medical underwriting!). 30-day free look.
- **Medicaid**: State/Federal joint program for low income. 60-month look-back on asset transfers.
- **NY State Partnership for LTC**: Protects 100% of personal assets from Medicaid spend-down after policy benefits are exhausted.
- **Workers' Comp vs. NY DBL**: Workers' Comp = ON-the-job (medical + wage); **NY DBL = OFF-the-job (cash wage replacement only, 26 weeks max, 7-day waiting period)**.
- **Detailed Notes**: [[medicare-vs-medicaid-vs-medigap]], [[long-term-care-and-ny-partnership]], [[new-york-disability-benefits-law-dbl]]

### F. New York State Specific Insurance Laws (~15% Weight)
- **Superintendent Powers**: Appointed by Governor/Senate; examines domestic insurers every 5 years; issues subpoenas, cease & desist, and fines up to $500/violation ($2,500 aggregate; $5,000 willful).
- **Licensing**: Agent (represents company, § 2103); Broker (represents insured, § 2104); Consultant (§ 2107). 15-hr CE every 2 years (minimum 3 hrs ethics). 30 days to report change of address or administrative actions.
- **Unfair Trade Practices**: Rebating strictly prohibited (gifts up to $25 allowed with logo); Twisting (misleading replacement); Defamation; Unfair claims.
- **Life & Health Guaranty Corporation**: $500,000 health protection limit; strict ban on using Guaranty Corp in advertising.
- **Detailed Note**: [[ny-specific-state-insurance-laws-mastery]]

## 5. Tricky Wording Guide for the Exam

- **"Must" vs. "May"**:
  - *"Must"* / *"Shall"* = Mandatory statutory requirement (e.g. "An insurer *must* provide claim forms within 15 days").
  - *"May"* = Discretionary or optional provision (e.g. "An insurer *may* include a Change of Occupation clause").
- **"First" / "Primary" vs. "Secondary"**:
  - In claims, look for who pays *first*. Under the Birthday Rule, the parent whose birthday falls *earliest in the calendar year* is primary.
  - In health insurance, Workers' Comp is ALWAYS primary over health policies or NY DBL!
- **"Best" vs. "Suitable"**:
  - In New York, producers are held to a fiduciary-like standard under Regulation 187: the recommendation must be in the consumer's *best interest*, not merely acceptable or suitable.
- **"Own Occupation" vs. "Any Occupation"**:
  - Watch for questions asking "Which definition is easiest for the claimant to qualify for?" Answer: **Own Occupation**.
  - Watch for questions asking "Which definition poses the lowest financial risk to the insurer?" Answer: **Any Occupation**.
- **"Guaranteed Renewable" vs. "Noncancelable"**:
  - Both guarantee that the policy cannot be canceled by the insurer (as long as premiums are paid).
  - The single test-maker distinction: In **Noncancelable**, premiums can **NEVER be increased**. In **Guaranteed Renewable**, premiums **CAN be increased on a class basis**.
- **"Reinstatement" (Sickness vs. Accident)**:
  - If a question asks when coverage begins for an *accident* after reinstatement: **Immediately**.
  - If a question asks when coverage begins for a *sickness*: **10 days**.
- **"Notice of Claim" vs. "Proof of Loss"**:
  - Notice of Claim = Telling the insurer you had a loss (**20 days**).
  - Proof of Loss = Submitting the itemized bills and doctor evidence (**90 days**).

## 6. Fast Multiple-Choice Method for Time Pressure

You have **120 minutes for 100 questions** = **1.2 minutes (72 seconds) per question**.

### The 4-Step "Strike & Solve" Method:
1. **Read the Final Sentence (The Actual Question) First**:
   - Long vignettes waste time. Jump straight to the question: e.g., *"What is the maximum time limit for submitting proof of loss?"*
   - Now scan the vignette only for the required variable (e.g., individual health policy).
2. **Watch for Negative Triggers ("EXCEPT", "NOT", "FALSE")**:
   - Mentally circle the word. Three answers are true; your job is to find the single false statement!
3. **Eliminate the 2 "Clown" Distractors Immediately**:
   - In almost every 4-choice question:
     - 1 choice is absurdly wrong.
     - 1 choice is from a completely different topic (e.g., life insurance rule or property rule).
     - Cross both out instantly. You now have a 50/50 probability.
4. **Identify the Statutory Distractor**:
   - Between the remaining two, test makers often include a generic federal rule vs. a specific New York rule. If the question mentions New York law, pick the New York statutory number (e.g., **36 months** continuation in NY vs. 18 months federal; **$25** gift cap in NY; **15-hr** CE in NY).
5. **Never Leave a Question Blank**:
   - No negative penalty for wrong answers on PSI exams. If unsure, mark your best guess, flag the question, and move on. Return only if time permits.
