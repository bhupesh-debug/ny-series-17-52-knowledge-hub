## 4. Taxation of Life & Health Insurance (CRITICAL RULES)

1. **Life Insurance Death Benefit**:
   - Received by beneficiary **100% Federal and NY State Income Tax-Free** when paid in a lump sum.
   - If paid under an interest or installment option, the principal is tax-free, but the **interest earned is taxable as ordinary income**.
2. **Cash Value Growth (Whole Life / UL)**:
   - Grows **tax-deferred**.
   - Withdrawals are taxed on a **FIFO basis** (First-In, First-Out): withdrawals up to cost basis (premiums paid) are tax-free return of capital; gains above basis are taxed as ordinary income.
   - Policy loans are **not taxable** while policy remains in force (unless policy is a Modified Endowment Contract - MEC).
3. **Modified Endowment Contracts (MECs)**:
   - Fails the **7-Pay Test** (too much premium paid in first 7 years).
   - Once a MEC, always a MEC.
   - Withdrawals/loans are taxed on a **LIFO basis** (Last-In, First-Out: interest/gains out first, ordinary income).
   - Pre-59½ distributions incur an additional **10% IRS penalty**.
   - Death benefit remains **income tax-free**!
4. **Disability Income Insurance**:
   - Individual policy paid with after-tax dollars = Benefits received are **100% Income Tax-Free**.
   - Group policy paid by employer (pre-tax business expense) = Benefits received by disabled employee are **100% Taxable as Ordinary Income**.
   - Contributory group: Taxed in exact proportion to the employer's contribution percentage.
