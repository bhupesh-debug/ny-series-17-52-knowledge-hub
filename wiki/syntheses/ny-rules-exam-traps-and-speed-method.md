# New York Rules, Exam Traps & Fast Question Method (Series 17-52)

## 1. Executive Summary & Source Authority
The **New York Series 17-52 Accident & Health Insurance Examination** tests both broad insurance principles and rigorous New York-specific statutes. Passing requires recognizing deceptive question framing, separating federal frameworks from state mandates, mastering time pacing (72 seconds/question), and applying the **Fast Question Method**.

- **Official Exam ID Clarification**:
  - **Series 17-52**: Accident & Health Insurance Agent / Broker ONLY (100 questions, 2 hours).
  - **Series 17-55**: Combined Life, Accident & Health Insurance Agent / Broker (150 questions, 3 hours).
- **Authority Sources**: [Official PSI Candidate Information Bulletin](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/7228) · [NYDFS Topic Locator](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2020/11/prel_ahab_2018.pdf)

## 2. Watch for These 5 Wording & Framing Traps

### Trap 1: "Must," "May," "Always," "Only" (Absolute vs. Conditional)
- **Check whether the rule is statutory (absolute) or contractual (conditional)**:
  - *"Must"* / *"Shall"*: Mandatory statutory rules. (e.g., Insurers *must* provide claim forms within 15 days; applicants *must* be given a 30-day free look on Medigap in NY).
  - *"May"*: Optional or permissive provisions. (e.g., An insurer *may* include an Illegal Occupation clause; an insurer *may* adjust benefits for Change of Occupation).
  - *"Always"* / *"Only"*: Distractors containing "always" or "only" are frequently wrong unless reflecting an explicit statutory mandate (e.g., "Medigap *only* supplements Original Medicare, never Medicare Advantage").

### Trap 2: "Best," "First," "Most Appropriate" (Pinpoint the Question Goal)
- Identify the exact target before reading answer choices:
  - *"What pays first?"* $\rightarrow$ Look for primary payer rules: Workers' Comp is primary over health; under COB Birthday Rule, the parent with the earliest birthday in the calendar year pays first.
  - *"What is best for the insured?"* $\rightarrow$ E.g., Noncancelable renewability is best for rate security; Own Occupation is best for claim eligibility.
  - *"What is the first step?"* $\rightarrow$ Notice of claim comes first (20 days), followed by claim forms (15 days), then proof of loss (90 days).

### Trap 3: Policy Language vs. General Intuition (The Policy Controls)
- General intuition suggests what seems "fair" or "generous"; **the policy contract definition strictly controls**:
  - *Example*: A doctor recommends a procedure, but the plan excludes experimental treatments. Intuition says "the doctor knows best, so pay it." **Exam rule**: The policy definition of *medical necessity* controls, and the insurer can legally deny the claim.
  - *Example*: A disabled business owner has a Business Overhead Expense (BOE) policy. Intuition says "he needs income to feed his family." **Exam rule**: BOE covers operating bills only; owner salary is strictly excluded!

### Trap 4: New York-Specific vs. Federal Frameworks
Never apply a general federal framework when the question specifies New York law:

| Federal Framework | New York-Specific Statutory Mandate |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Federal COBRA**: Applies to 20+ workers; 18 months for termination. | **NY Mini-COBRA**: Applies to **1–19 workers**; extends continuation up to **36 MONTHS**! |
| **Federal ACA Dependent Age**: Extends coverage to age 26. | **NY Age 29 Law**: Young adults can elect continuation/dependent rider up to **age 29**. |
| **Federal ACA Rating**: Allows 3:1 age rating and tobacco rating. | **NY Pure Community Rating**: **No age, gender, or health rating** in individual/small group! |
| **Federal Medigap Open Enrollment**: 6-month window at age 65. | **NY Medigap Rule**: **Continuous year-round open enrollment** & community rating! |
| **Federal Disability (SSDI)**: Severe total disability, 5-month wait. | **NY DBL**: Non-work only, 50% AWW capped at **$170/wk, 26 weeks, 7-day wait, cash only**! |

### Trap 5: Life vs. Accident & Health Scopes
- **Series 17-52 is strictly Accident & Health**: Do not spend time memorizing life nonforfeiture options, suicide clauses, or annuity surrender charges.
- However, recognize the basic structural party roles:
  - **Policyowner**: Controls the contract, exercises rights, pays premiums.
  - **Insured**: The person whose health or body is covered.
  - **Beneficiary**: Receives benefits (e.g. AD&D death benefit).

## 3. Fast Question Method ("The 5-Step Strike")

For a **100-question, 2-hour exam**, your budget is **72 seconds per question**:

```text
[Step 1] Name the Topic:
         Is this Medical Plans, Disability, Policy Provisions, Group, Government, or NY Law?
   │
[Step 2] Spot the Decisive Word:
         Own vs. Any Occ? In vs. Out-of-network? Master vs. Certificate?
         Guaranteed Renewable vs. Noncancelable? Medicare vs. Medicaid? DBL vs. Workers' Comp?
   │
[Step 3] State the Rule in ONE Sentence:
         (e.g., "Guaranteed renewable means the company cannot cancel, but can raise rates by class.")
   │
[Step 4] Cross Out Answers that Change the Rule or Add an Absolute:
         Eliminate distractors that add "always," change numbers, or promise "all medical bills."
   │
[Step 5] Choose the Answer that Fits the Stated Facts:
         Select the option that strictly matches the legal or contractual definition,
         NOT what sounds most generous!
```

### Time Pacing Strategy:
- **First Pass (Questions 1–100)**: Answer all straightforward definitional and numerical questions immediately (aim for 30–45 seconds each).
- **Mark & Move**: If a question requires reading a long vignette or multi-step calculation, mark/flag it and keep your momentum.
- **Second Pass**: Return to flagged questions with the banked surplus time.
- **Zero Blanks**: Never leave any question unanswered; PSI exams carry **no penalty for guessing**.
