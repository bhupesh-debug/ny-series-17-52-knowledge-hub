# Superintendent of Financial Services (NYS DFS)

## 1. Overview and Appointment
The **Superintendent of Financial Services** is the chief administrative officer of the **[[new-york-state-department-of-financial-services|New York State Department of Financial Services (NYS DFS)]]**.

- **Appointment**: Appointed by the **Governor of New York**, by and with the advice and consent of the **New York State Senate**.
- **Tenure**: Serves at the pleasure of the Governor.
- **Governing Statute**: New York Financial Services Law § 202 & New York Insurance Law (NYIL) § 301.

---

## 2. Core Powers and Duties (HIGH FREQUENCY EXAM TEST)

The Superintendent has broad administrative, regulatory, investigatory, and quasi-judicial authority, including:
1. **Regulate & Supervise**: Transact the business of the department and ensure compliance with New York Insurance Law.
2. **Issue, Suspend, Revoke, and Refuse Licenses**: For agents, brokers, adjusters, consultants, and reinsurance intermediaries.
3. **Approve Policy Forms & Rates**: Review and approve insurance policy contracts, endorsements, riders, and rate filings.
4. **Conduct Financial & Market Conduct Examinations**:
   - Authorized to examine domestic, foreign, and alien insurers.
   - Domestic life insurers must be examined at least **once every 5 years** (NYIL § 309).
   - Insurers must maintain all transaction records for at least **6 years** and make them available upon request.
5. **Issue Cease and Desist Orders**: To halt unlawful, unsafe, or deceptive practices immediately.
6. **Subpoena Powers**: Subpoena witnesses, compel testimony, administer oaths, and require the production of books, records, and accounts.
7. **Impose Fines and Civil Penalties**:
   - General penalty: Up to **$500 per violation** (or up to **$2,500 aggregate** for series of non-willful violations).
   - Unfair trade practices / Twisting / Rebating: Can trigger individual penalties up to **$5,000 per violation**.

## 3. What the Superintendent CANNOT Do (EXAM TRAPS)

> [!WARNING]
> **Exam Distractor Alert**: Test questions frequently ask: *"Which of the following is NOT within the authority of the Superintendent?"*
>
> 1. **The Superintendent DOES NOT MAKE OR PASS LAWS**: Laws are enacted exclusively by the **New York State Legislature**. The Superintendent *enforces* statutory law and promulgates *rules/regulations* (11 NYCRR) to carry out the law.
> 2. **The Superintendent DOES NOT PROSECUTE CRIMINALS DIRECTLY**: The Superintendent can hold civil administrative hearings and impose civil fines, but criminal prosecutions are referred to the **New York State Attorney General** or local District Attorneys.
> 3. **The Superintendent CANNOT ARBITRARILY SET PREMIUM AMOUNTS**: The Superintendent approves or disapproves rate filings based on statutory standards (adequate, not excessive, not unfairly discriminatory), but does not run the private actuarial pricing of insurance companies.

---

## 4. Disciplinary Procedures & Due Process (NYIL § 2110)

Before revoking or suspending a license (or imposing a fine in lieu of suspension), the Superintendent must:
- Provide **written notice** specifying the charges.
- Give the licensee **at least 10 days' advance notice** of a formal administrative hearing.
- Following revocation, the person is barred from obtaining any new insurance license in New York for **at least 1 year** from the date of final revocation.
