# New York State Department of Financial Services (NYS DFS)

## 1. Overview and Legal Authority
The **New York State Department of Financial Services (NYS DFS)** was established on October 3, 2011, pursuant to the New York Financial Services Law, through the merger of the historic **New York State Insurance Department** (founded in 1859) and the **New York State Banking Department** (founded in 1851).

NYS DFS is the primary regulatory agency responsible for supervising all state-chartered banks, financial institutions, insurance companies, producers (agents, brokers, consultants, adjusters), and other financial service entities operating in the State of New York.

---

## 2. Core Mandate and Responsibilities
- **Consumer Protection**: Guarding the public against predatory, misleading, insolvent, or fraudulent financial practices.
- **Solvency Oversight**: Ensuring that insurance companies and financial institutions maintain statutory reserves and remain financially sound to meet contractual obligations to policyholders.
- **Licensing and Regulation**: Administering examinations, issuing and renewing licenses for insurance agents (NYIL § 2103), brokers (NYIL § 2104), and consultants (NYIL § 2107).
- **Market Conduct Examinations**: Investigating complaints, conducting routine and targeted audits of domestic, foreign, and alien insurers.
- **Promulgation of Regulations**: Enforcing Title 11 of the New York Codes, Rules and Regulations (11 NYCRR), including [[new-york-insurance-regulation-60-replacement|Regulation 60 (Replacement)]] and [[new-york-insurance-regulation-187-suitability|Regulation 187 (Best Interest)]].

## 3. High-Yield Exam Facts: DFS Classifications of Insurers

| Classification Type | Definition | Exam Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Domestic Insurer** | Formed and incorporated under the laws of **New York State**. | Must be examined by DFS at least **once every 5 years**. |
| **Foreign Insurer** | Incorporated under the laws of **another US State** or territory (e.g. Delaware, Ohio). | Regulated by home state for charter, but must have NY Certificate of Authority to sell in NY. |
| **Alien Insurer** | Incorporated under the laws of **another country** (e.g. Canada, UK, Bermuda). | Must establish a trusteed US branch asset deposit to operate in NY. |
| **Authorized (Admitted)** | Holds a valid **Certificate of Authority** issued by the Superintendent to transact insurance business in NY. | Only authorized insurers are covered by the [[life-and-health-insurance-company-guaranty-corporation-of-new-york|Guaranty Corporation]]. |
| **Unauthorized (Non-Admitted)**| Does not hold a NY Certificate of Authority (e.g., surplus lines). | Unlawful for standard agents to solicit or sell without surplus lines broker license. |
