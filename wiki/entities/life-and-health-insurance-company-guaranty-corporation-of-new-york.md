# The Life Insurance Company Guaranty Corporation of New York

## 1. Overview and Legal Authority
Governed by **Article 77 of the New York Insurance Law (NYIL § 7701–7718)**, the **Life Insurance Company Guaranty Corporation of New York** (often referred to simply as the "Guaranty Corporation") protects New York resident policyholders, beneficiaries, and annuitants in the event an authorized life or health insurance company becomes financially insolvent or impaired.

All insurance companies authorized to transact life insurance, annuities, or health insurance in New York **MUST BE MEMBERS** of the Guaranty Corporation as a condition of their authority to conduct business in the state.

---

## 2. Funding Mechanism
- The Guaranty Corporation is **NOT funded by public tax dollars**.
- It is funded through **post-insolvency assessments** levied on all solvent member insurance companies in proportion to the net direct premiums they collect in New York across three statutory accounts:
  1. Life Insurance Account
  2. Health Insurance Account
  3. Annuity Account

---

## 3. Statutory Coverage Limits in New York (CRITICAL EXAM NUMBERS)

Under NYIL § 7708, the maximum liability of the Guaranty Corporation with respect to any **one individual life** (regardless of the number of policies held with the insolvent insurer) is:

| Coverage Line | Maximum Statutory Protection Limit |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Life Insurance Death Benefit** | Up to **$500,000** in death benefits |
| **Life Insurance Net Cash Surrender / Withdrawal Value** | Up to **$100,000** in cash value |
| **Annuity Benefits (Present Value of Annuity)** | Up to **$500,000** in present value of annuity benefits |
| **Health / Medical Expense Insurance** | Up to **$500,000** in health benefits |
| **Disability Income / Long-Term Care** | Up to **$500,000** in disability/LTC benefits |
| **Aggregate Maximum Liability Per Individual** | **$500,000** total across all lines (except if life death benefit is involved, still capped at $500k) |

## 4. Unlawful Advertising Rule (CRITICAL ETHICS TRAP)

> [!WARNING]
> **Strict Prohibition on Marketing with the Guaranty Corporation (NYIL § 7718)**:
> It is an **unlawful unfair trade practice** for any insurer, agent, broker, or producer to use the existence of the Guaranty Corporation in ANY sales presentation, advertisement, or solicitation to induce a prospect to purchase a policy!
>
> *Exam Question Pattern*: "An agent tells a prospective client: 'Don't worry about this company's financial rating—New York's Guaranty Corporation backs your policy up to $500,000!' What violation has the agent committed?"
> - **Answer**: **Unlawful Advertisement / Prohibited Sales Practice**. The existence of the Guaranty Corporation must never be used as a selling point or guarantee.
